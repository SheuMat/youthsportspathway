Who We Are

Sheffield's Premier Youth Sports Development Company

Founded by Eddy Brownell and Nicholas Lewis, Youth Sports Pathway exists to make high-quality sports opportunities accessible to every child in Sheffield. From grassroots sessions to competitive pathways, we develop confidence, skill, and character through sport.
