Our Story

About Youth Sports Pathway

Built on the belief that every young person in Sheffield deserves access to world-class sports development, regardless of background or ability.

Our Mission

Unlocking Potential Through Sport

Youth Sports Pathway was founded with a single purpose: to make high-quality sports development accessible to every child and young person in Sheffield. We believe sport is one of the most powerful tools for building confidence, discipline, resilience, and community.

From grassroots weekly sessions to competitive school leagues and elite pathways, we meet young athletes wherever they are on their journey and help them become the best version of themselves — on and off the court.

The Founders

Meet the Visionaries Behind YSP

Eddy Brownell

Co-Founder & Director

Eddy brings years of coaching experience and a deep passion for youth development in Sheffield. As a former player himself, he understands the journey from the inside out, and is committed to building structures that give every young athlete a real pathway forward.

Nicholas Lewis

Co-Founder & Director

Nicholas leads the operational and community side of YSP, forging strong relationships with schools, local authorities, and partner organisations. His vision for community-based sports development has shaped YSP's reputation as Sheffield's most trusted youth sports provider.
