Eddy Brownell

Co-Founder & Head Coach

Eddy Brownell is a former professional basketball player with eight years of professional playing experience, including seven seasons with the Sheffield Sharks. A member of the Sharks' 2016 BBL Playoff-winning team, Eddy brings his playing and coaching experience into developing young players through Youth Sports Pathway.
