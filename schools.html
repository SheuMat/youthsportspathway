School Programmes

Curriculum-aligned PE delivery, after-school clubs, enrichment days and competitive events — tailored for primary and secondary schools across Sheffield.

What We Offer

Programmes Built Around Your School

Every YSP school programme is flexible, fully resourced and delivered by qualified coaches who understand the school environment.

🏃

PE Delivery

High-quality PE lessons delivered by YSP coaches, freeing up teacher time while giving pupils an exceptional sporting experience. Aligned to the national curriculum.

🏀

After-School Clubs

Basketball, dodgeball and multi-sport clubs running after school. Fun, structured sessions that keep young people active and engaged beyond the school day.

🎉

Enrichment Days

Full-day or half-day enrichment events bringing energy and excitement to your school calendar. Perfect for sports days, reward days or curriculum enrichment weeks.

📚

Curriculum Support

Sport-linked literacy, numeracy and PSHE activities integrated into PE sessions. Helping schools meet wider curriculum goals through the power of sport.

🎯

Dodgeball Programme

One of Sheffield's most popular school sports activities. Dodgeball develops teamwork, coordination and explosive movement — and pupils absolutely love it.

🏈

Flag Football

An exciting, non-contact introduction to American football. Develops agility, strategy and communication skills — ideal for mixed-ability groups.
