After-School Clubs

How It Works

📅

Half-Term Blocks

One 60-minute session each week, run in 6–8 week half-term blocks. Parents pay upfront for the block: 6 weeks £30, 7 weeks £35, 8 weeks £40.

👥

Group Sizes

Minimum 10, maximum 25 participants. One coach for up to 15 players, two coaches for 16–25 players.

🏀

Years 1–3: Foundation Basketball

Enjoyment, movement, confidence, coordination, ball skills, simple decision-making and small-sided games.

📈

Years 4–6: Development Basketball

Ball handling, finishing, shooting, 1v1 play, passing, movement, spacing, decision-making and game understanding.

YSP Development Pathway

Where School Basketball Can Lead

YSP is an independent basketball development organisation, not a club. Players from any club or team can train and develop with us.

1

School Basketball

After-school clubs and PE delivery introduce pupils to basketball in a fun, structured way.

2

YSP Community Basketball

Weekly community sessions open to all, wherever players go to school.

3

YSP Skills & Development

Additional coaching to build skills, confidence and game understanding.

4

Further Basketball Opportunities

We help players and families find the right next step, which could be local clubs, competitive or National League basketball, performance programmes, college or sixth-form, university, USA opportunities where appropriate, or other external opportunities.
