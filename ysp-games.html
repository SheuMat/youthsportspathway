Annual Competition

YSP Games

Sheffield's premier inter-school sports competition. A structured pathway from school qualifiers all the way to the Grand Final.

The Journey

Competition Pathway

Every school gets a route to the final. Four stages, one champion.

01

School Qualifiers

In-school tournaments to select your top team. Organised by YSP coaches within your school.

02

Zone Finals

Top teams from each area of Sheffield compete in zone-based competition events.

03

City Semi-Finals

Zone winners battle it out in the city semi-finals — hosted at a premium Sheffield venue.

04

Grand Final 🏆

The showpiece event of the YSP calendar. The best teams in Sheffield compete for the title.

Why Enter?

More Than a Competition

The YSP Games give young people an experience they'll never forget — the thrill of representing their school, competing at a high level, and being part of something bigger than themselves.

  • ✓ Medals and trophies for finalists
  • ✓ Professional photography and video
  • ✓ Individual player awards and recognition
  • ✓ Elite player scouting opportunities
  • ✓ Free for all participating schools
