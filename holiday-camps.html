Holiday Activities & Food Programme

Free Funded Places via HAF

YSP is an approved Holiday Activities and Food (HAF) provider. This means children in receipt of free school meals can access our holiday camps completely free of charge — including a nutritious hot lunch each day.

If your child is eligible, contact your school or Sheffield City Council to receive your HAF voucher code, then book through us.
