Membership Terms

  • Membership is for the selected programme and session day(s) only. Grassroots and Development are separate memberships.
  • Payments are collected monthly by Direct Debit through GoCardless and are protected by the Direct Debit Guarantee.
  • Setting up a Direct Debit is not the same as a payment being taken. We'll confirm your start date, collection date and cancellation terms by email before your first payment.

We use these details only to manage your booking and contact you about your child's sessions. Information is stored securely and is not shared except with service providers needed to run our programmes. See our privacy information.

Where online payment is open you'll be taken to GoCardless's secure page to authorise your Direct Debit. YSP never sees your bank details.
